# NMTA Mock Examinations (practice set)

Sixteen practice papers for **New Media Technology and Analytics** (20001920076), Fall 2026, Tongji SEM. They match the final exam's structure, marks and exclusions, and use new fictional scenarios only.

## Structure of every paper

| Part | Format | Marks |
|---|---|---|
| A | 15 multiple-choice questions | 30 |
| B | 5 short answers (3–5 sentences each) | 30 |
| C | 2 cases with data exhibits | 40 |
| **Total** | 90 minutes | **100** |

Formulas needed for a question are printed beside it. No program-code writing is required. Week 9+ content and ethical/legal topics are excluded, matching the official exam scope.

## How the set is organised

- Each paper covers Weeks 1–8.
- Across the 16 papers, every examined topic of the course is tested at least once.
- No question is reused. Scenarios and numbers differ from the official mock, from the study book practice questions, and from the course assignments and group project.
- Answer letters in Part A are roughly balanced across (a)–(d).

## Suggested use

1. Sit one paper under exam conditions (90 minutes, one A4 sheet of notes, non-programmable calculator).
2. Mark yourself with the matching answer key in `../mock_exam_answer_keys/`.
3. Read the marking guide carefully: marks reward distinct points of reasoning, not wording that matches the sample.
4. Keep a short list of topics you missed and revisit those sections of the study book before the next paper.

A realistic plan before the Thursday exam is three to four papers, marked carefully, rather than racing through all sixteen.
