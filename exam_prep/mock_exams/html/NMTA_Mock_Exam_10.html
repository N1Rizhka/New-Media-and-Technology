A2 (2 marks). A platform tests whether a streamer's home platform (3 values) is linked to content category (3 values). The test gives χ2 = 8.2. Critical values at α = 0.05: df = 3: 7.81; df = 4: 9.49. Which conclusion is correct?
df = (rows − 1) × (columns − 1)
  1. (a) No evidence of a link at this level, because 8.2 is below 9.49 with df = 4.
  2. (b) Evidence of a link, because 8.2 is above the critical value 7.81 with df = 3.
  3. (c) The two attributes are proven unrelated, because 8.2 is below 9.49 with df = 4.
  4. (d) Evidence of a link, because any positive χ2 shows that observed counts differ.

A4 (2 marks). In 300 baskets with minimum support 10%, the pairs of {A, B, C} have counts AB 41, AC 35 and BC 28. What does the Apriori principle say about {A, B, C}?
minimum count = minimum support × number of baskets
  1. (a) Discard it without counting, because BC falls below 30 baskets.
  2. (b) Keep it as frequent, because two of its three pairs reach 30.
  3. (c) Count it, because the average of its pair counts exceeds 30.
  4. (d) Keep it, because its count is the sum of its three pair counts.
A5 (2 marks). For {podcast mic} → {pop filter}, confidence is .30 and lift is 2.4. What is the lift of {pop filter} → {podcast mic}?
lift(X → Y) = s(X ∪ Y) / (s(X) × s(Y))
  1. (a) 0.42, because reversing the rule inverts the lift (1 / 2.4).
  2. (b) It cannot be found without the reverse rule's confidence.
  3. (c) 0.30, because the lift of a reversed rule equals its confidence.
  4. (d) 2.4, because lift does not depend on the direction of the arrow.

A9 (2 marks). A row shows coefficient 0.84, standard error 0.21 and a printed p of 0.000. Which reading is correct?
t = coefficient / standard error
  1. (a) t = 0.25, so the estimate sits a quarter of a noise unit away from zero.
  2. (b) t = 4.00, and p = 0.000 means p is exactly zero, so the link is certain.
  3. (c) t = 4.00, and p = 0.000 means p is below 0.0005, so reject H0.
  4. (d) t = 1.05, the coefficient plus its error, so do not reject H0.

A11 (2 marks). A silhouette-by-k table for a gaming app starts at k = 2, while its elbow table starts at k = 1. Why is there no average silhouette for k = 1?
s = (b − a) / max(a, b)
  1. (a) With one group, a is always zero, so the silhouette is undefined.
  2. (b) Software skips k = 1 by convention, but it could be computed as +1.
  3. (c) With one group there is no other group, so b cannot be measured.
  4. (d) With one group every viewer is misfiled, so the score is always −1.

A15 (2 marks). P, Q, R and S are all tied to each other (six ties). S is also tied to T, and T is tied to U. There are no other ties. Which statement is correct?
Betweenness of v = number of pairs of other accounts whose shortest path passes through v (if a pair has several equally short paths, each path carries an equal share of the point).
  1. (a) P has a higher betweenness than T, because P has three ties and T has two.
  2. (b) S has betweenness 4, because it holds four ties.
  3. (c) T has betweenness 0, because it sits at the edge of the map.
  4. (d) T has betweenness 4, even though P, with more ties, has 0.
