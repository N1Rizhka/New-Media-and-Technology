A5 (2 marks). A rule has support .015, confidence .95 and lift 1.03. Which verdict and reason fit best?
  1. (a) Kill: the lift is below 1, so the two items substitute for each other.
  2. (b) Ignore: few baskets back the confidence, and lift near 1 shows no signal.
  3. (c) Keep and act: a confidence of .95 means the rule is nearly always right in use.
  4. (d) Keep and act: any lift above 1 means an association worth acting on.
A6 (2 marks). A comment-spam model gives one comment a spam probability of 0.62. The team has been deleting every comment that scores 0.5 or higher, and now raises the threshold to 0.7 without changing the model. What happens to this comment?
verdict = delete if score ≥ t; keep if score < t
  1. (a) The model must be retrained before the comment can receive a new verdict.
  2. (b) Its score is pushed down below 0.7, so the comment is now kept.
  3. (c) Its score stays 0.62, but its verdict changes from delete to keep.
  4. (d) It is still deleted, because 0.62 is above the original cutoff of 0.5.

A15 (2 marks). In a network of 13 accounts, account M has a distance sum of 24 and account N has a distance sum of 30. Which statement is correct?
Closeness = (n − 1) / distance sum, where n = number of accounts and the distance sum is the total of the shortest hop distances to every other account.
  1. (a) M's closeness is 0.50 and N's is 0.40, so M reaches everyone faster.
  2. (b) N's closeness is higher, because a larger distance sum means wider reach.
  3. (c) M's closeness is 2.00 and N's is 2.50, so N reaches everyone faster.
  4. (d) M's closeness is 0.54 and N's is 0.43, so M reaches everyone faster.
