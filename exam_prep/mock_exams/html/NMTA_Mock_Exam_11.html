A7 (2 marks). On the threshold curve, a new vendor's filter at its chosen setting has a catch rate of 0.40 and a false-alarm rate of 0.40. How should this point be read?
catch rate = TP / (TP + FN); false-alarm rate = FP / (FP + TN)
  1. (a) It lies in the top-left corner: it catches most spam with very few false alarms.
  2. (b) It is a balanced filter: equal rates mean the two errors are perfectly priced.
  3. (c) It sits on the diagonal: it is no better than a coin flip at catching spam.
  4. (d) It cannot be judged until the filter's accuracy is also reported.

A15 (2 marks). A brand account H is tied to six fan accounts, and the fans have no other ties. What are the closeness scores of H and of one fan, to two decimal places?
Closeness = (n − 1) / distance sum, where n = number of accounts and the distance sum is the total of the shortest hop distances to every other account.
  1. (a) H 6.00; fan 1.00
  2. (b) H 1.00; fan 0.17
  3. (c) H 0.86; fan 0.55
  4. (d) H 1.00; fan 0.55
