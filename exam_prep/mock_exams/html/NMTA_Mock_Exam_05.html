A5 (2 marks). A phone-accessory store has 6,000 customers. 2,400 buy a phone case, 4,800 buy a screen protector and 1,800 buy both. Which statement about {phone case} → {screen protector} is correct?
s(X → Y) = σ(X ∪ Y) / N; c(X → Y) = σ(X ∪ Y) / σ(X)
  1. (a) The rule is strong, because its confidence of .75 is well above the .50 mark.

  3. (c) Case buyers take a protector less often than customers overall (.75 vs .80).
  4. (d) The rule should be bundled, because both items are bought very often.

A13 (2 marks). In a collection of 50 headphone reviews, the word "earbuds" appears in all 50. It appears 6 times in review r7. What is its TF-IDF weight in r7?
TF-IDF(t, d) = TF(t, d) × IDF(t), with IDF(t) = ln(N / df(t)); TF = number of times t appears in d; N = number of documents; df(t) = number of documents containing t.
  1. (a) 0, because IDF = ln(50/50) = ln(1) = 0.
  2. (b) 6, because a common word's weight equals its count.
  3. (c) 23.47, because 6 × ln(50) rewards the repetition.
  4. (d) 1.79, because ln(6) measures repetition only.

Change in fitted value = coefficient × change in feature

B4 (6 marks). Pinecrest Coffee wants to (i) count what its reviews are about and (ii) score their sentiment. A proposed bilingual stop list is: the, a, and, of, not, never, 的, 了, 在, 不, 没, 很. In a sample of 200 reviews labeled negative, 64 contain 不 or 没.

Percentage = reviews containing the terms / all reviews in the sample × 100.
B5 (6 marks). Saltmarsh Coffee's eight store managers pass messages along these undirected ties: S1–S2, S1–S3, S2–S3, S3–S4, S4–S5, S5–S6, S5–S7, S7–S8. S3's distance sum is 15. (i) Compute the distance sums and closeness of S4, S5 and S8 (three decimal places). (ii) A batch recall notice must reach all managers as fast as possible, starting from one manager. Recommend where to start and explain what the results show about S4, which holds only two ties.
Closeness = (n − 1) / distance sum, where n = number of accounts and the distance sum is the total of the shortest hop distances to every other account.
