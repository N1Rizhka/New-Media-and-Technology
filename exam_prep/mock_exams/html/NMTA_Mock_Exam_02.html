A5 (2 marks). Rules that point at a shop's best-selling item tend to show fairly high confidence, yet their lift stays close to 1. Why?
lift(X → Y) = c(X → Y) / s(Y)
  1. (a) Confidence is computed over all baskets, while lift is computed over the X baskets only.
  2. (b) Lift ignores the item's popularity and measures only how often X itself is bought.
  3. (c) Confidence rises with the item's popularity; lift divides by that same base rate.
  4. (d) High support always pulls lift below 1, so popular items are always substitutes.
