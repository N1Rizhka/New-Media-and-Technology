W3 · Association rules

Three numbers s · c · lift

s(I)=σ(I)/N · c(X→Y)=σ(X∪Y)/σ(X) · lift = c / s(Y) = s(X∪Y)/(s(X)s(Y))

X,Y disjoint. Frequent if s ≥ minsup. Support symmetric; confidence directional; lift symmetric. Basket = presence 1/0 (not quantity); support counts orders, not units.

LiftWhat it showsAction
< 1substitutes: X makes Y less likely than chancedon’t push the pair
= 1independent: X tells you nothing about Y (chance level)ignore as association
> 1go together: Y more likely when X is presentcandidate to act on

1 is chance. High lift (e.g. matcha→whisk 7.80) = strong “buy together” signal → bundle/promote. Low lift / <1 = substitutes or noise → don’t push. Lift ≈1 with high support = rides along with everything (doilies decoy) → predicts nothing.

Overfit & penalties bias²+var OLS/Ridge/LASSO

E[(y−ŷ)²] = bias² + variance + noise · Ridge: SSE+λΣβ² · LASSO: SSE+λΣ|β|

Flexible → high variance; simple → high bias. Ridge shrinks, rarely to 0. LASSO can set coef = 0 (selection). Choose λ on validation; strongest λ ≠ automatically best. Zero coef = no direct role in this fit, not “never matters”.

W6 · Clustering

K-means basics

Choose k → seed centroids → assign nearest → move to mean → repeat. Standardise first: z=(x−μ)/σ.

Restart ≠ Stability
Restart: several random seeds on the same data; keep the run with lowest WCSS. = “best among these starts”, not proof of global best.
Stability: re-run on resamples + new seeds; ask whether the same segments reappear (size + profile, not just the count). Stable → safe to plan campaigns; unstable → “a story about the seed”.

WCSS = Σ d² to own centroid (always falls as k↑) · s = (b−a)/max(a,b)

a = mean dist. to own cluster; b = mean dist. to nearest other. Zones: near +1 well inside · ≈0 on the boundary · negative closer to another group (misfiled). Elbow + silhouette agree when structure is real.

Community detection community modularity Q Louvain

Community = denser inside than outside. Finds natural segments / regional pockets / fraud rings in the ties — nobody declares the groups. = Kotler’s measurable & reachable segment found in the data.

Q = Σ_c [ e_c/m − (d_c/(2m))² ]

Modularity Q answers: “are these groups tighter than chance?” = share of edges inside communities − share expected if ties were random. Higher Q = clearer cut. Q ≈ 0 ≈ random; Q ≈ 0.3+ real structure. Corner split into two fours: Q = .367.

Louvain (3 steps): (1) every node starts alone → (2) move each node to the group that raises Q most → (3) merge & repeat until nothing moves. Finds k itself; may swallow small groups. Label propagation: labels spread until stable.
